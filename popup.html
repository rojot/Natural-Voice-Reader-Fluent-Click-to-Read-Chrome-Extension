🔊 Natural Voice Reader

Floating English text-to-speech controller with click-any-word reading & auto-scroll.

Quick Guide:
• Move Player: Drag the top bar anywhere.
• Click to Read: Click any word on the webpage to start reading from that spot.
• Skip: Use Prev/Next Line or Prev/Next Para.
• Speed: Use − / + or the slider (0.5x–3.0x).
